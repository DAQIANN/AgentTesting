Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. This energy is stored in glucose molecules, which serve as fuel for the organism. The process occurs primarily in the leaves of plants and requires three main ingredients: carbon dioxide, water, and sunlight. Chlorophyll, the green pigment in plant cells, captures light energy and initiates the reaction. Oxygen is released as a byproduct, making photosynthesis essential for life on Earth.
